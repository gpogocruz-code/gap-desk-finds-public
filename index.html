GAP Desk Finds Analytics

GAP Desk Finds Analytics is a private content analytics dashboard used to understand the performance of content published by GAP Desk Finds.

Pinterest integration

The Pinterest integration is used only to access analytics and information belonging to the authenticated GAP Desk Finds account.

The dashboard may display:

  • Pins and boards owned by GAP Desk Finds
  • Organic impressions
  • Saves
  • Pin clicks
  • Outbound clicks
  • Engagement and content performance

The application does not publish Pins, manage advertising campaigns, or access Pins belonging to unrelated Pinterest users.

Purpose

Data is used exclusively to create private analytics reports and improve the content planning workflow of GAP Desk Finds.
