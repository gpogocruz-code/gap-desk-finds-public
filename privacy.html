Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GAP Desk Finds Analytics handles information obtained through its Pinterest integration.

1. Purpose of the application

GAP Desk Finds Analytics is a private analytics dashboard used by GAP Desk Finds to analyze the performance of its own social media content.

2. Information accessed from Pinterest

With authorization from the authenticated Pinterest account, the application may access:

  • Basic account information
  • Pins owned by the authenticated account
  • Boards owned by the authenticated account
  • Organic analytics and performance metrics
  • Impressions, saves, Pin clicks and outbound clicks

3. How the information is used

Pinterest data is used solely to generate private analytics, performance reports and content insights for GAP Desk Finds.

The information is not sold, rented or used for advertising profiling.

4. Authentication

The application uses Pinterest's official authorization mechanisms. GAP Desk Finds Analytics does not request or store a user's Pinterest password.

5. Data storage and security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored securely on the application's backend and are not exposed in the public dashboard. Only the minimum information necessary for analytics is processed.

6. Third-party access

Pinterest data obtained through the application is not sold or shared with unrelated third parties.

7. Disconnecting Pinterest

Authorization can be revoked from Pinterest account settings. When access is revoked, the application will no longer retrieve new Pinterest information.

8. Data deletion

A request may be made to remove stored Pinterest-related analytics information associated with the application.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the functionality of GAP Desk Finds Analytics changes. The latest version will remain available at this URL.

10. Contact

For privacy or data deletion requests, contact GAP Desk Finds through its official account contact channels.
